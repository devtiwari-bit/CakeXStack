# CakeXStack

**CakeXStack** is a free, addictive one-tap cake stacking game by **Ved Games Production**.

Drop each cake layer onto the tower and land it dead-on. Hit five perfect drops in a row and the cakes start growing bigger. Behind the tower, a night city comes alive with cats hopping on the rooftops, crows flying past, a neighbor waving from a lit window, and funny billboards.

**Play now:** https://devtiwari-bit.github.io/CakeXStack/

## Features

- One-tap gameplay that is easy to learn and hard to put down
- Perfect-drop streaks that make your cake grow bigger
- Easy, Normal, and Hard difficulty levels
- Speed ramps up as your tower grows
- Animated city background with cats, crows, and window characters
- Best score saved on your device
- Works in any modern browser on desktop and mobile
- Can be added to your phone's home screen (iPhone and Android)

## How to play

- **Phone:** Tap anywhere to drop the cake layer.
- **Computer:** Click anywhere, or press **Space**.
- Choose **Easy**, **Normal**, or **Hard** before you start.

Your best score is saved in your browser, so it stays between visits on the same device.

## Put it online with GitHub Pages

1. Upload all the files in this folder to the root of your repository.
2. In the repository, go to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, set the source to **Deploy from a branch**, choose the `main` branch and the `/ (root)` folder, then save.
4. After a minute or two, the game will be live at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/CakeXStack/`.

If your address is different, update the links in `index.html`, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml` to match it.

## Search engines

To help people find the game on Google:

1. Open [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) and add the website.
2. Verify ownership (the HTML tag method is the easiest).
3. Go to **Sitemaps**, enter `sitemap.xml`, and click **Submit**.

Google can take a few days to a few weeks to show the game in results.
